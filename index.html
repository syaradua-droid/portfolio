About

I come to product design from a habit of asking what a screen is actually for before deciding what it looks like. TuneVault grew out of real interview transcripts and personas that had to earn their place in the flow; GloveWorks grew out of direct stakeholder discovery with a real client; GameMind is a self-directed concept — a problem I wanted to design for end to end, with no client and no formal research behind it yet. Different inputs, same discipline — never a template that got reskinned.

I've completed the Google UX Design Certificate and I'm looking for a first full-time design role where research and systems thinking are treated as part of the design work, not a separate step before it.
